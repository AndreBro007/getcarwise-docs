## The common commercial journey

Buyer question → useful answer/shortlist → preferred model or actual vehicle → one concrete unresolved buying question → suitable tracked Edmunds destination → buyer chooses dealer contact → eligible submitted action → affiliate approval.

A preference for a model is not the same as readiness to contact a dealer. Connect the recommendation to a real car/dealer and something worth asking. Availability remains useful, but several other honest reasons exist:

| Buyer outcome | Actual unresolved question | Reason for dealer contact |
|---|---|---|
| Compare two finalists | Does the actual full price change the comparison? | Ask for an itemised out-the-door price. |
| Needs a particular feature | Is it installed on this exact car? | Ask for confirmation/build-sheet evidence. |
| Concerned about used-car condition | Are service records available and will the dealer allow an independent inspection? | Request documents or discuss an inspection. |
| Family/physical fit | Does their own seating, cargo or accessibility setup work? | Arrange a practical fit check/test drive. |
| Ready for a preferred listing | Is it still available and can they see it? | Confirm availability and arrange a visit. |
| Chosen model, no exact listing | Which local examples match the important requirements? | Browse relevant Edmunds options, choose one, then enquire. |

Do not manufacture missing information, scarcity or fear to obtain enquiries. Show known facts and genuine uncertainties. The recommendation must remain useful if a buyer decides to avoid a car.

These are buyer motivations and possible copy, not claims that the present Edmunds destination supports a separate button/form for every request. Check the actual permitted destination and form before promising messaging, quote delivery, records or appointment booking. A direct dealer email/phone contact outside the tracked qualifying journey must not be counted as affiliate success. A tracked click alone is not a payable lead.

Use approved affiliate creative and disclose the commercial relationship. Keep the approved asset label/destination accurate; place helpful buyer reasoning beside it rather than invent an unapproved label or a nonexistent dealer-request feature.

## Profiles

### 1. Reddit

**Natural behaviour:** Buyer situations, shortlist debates, practical owner experience and focused questions.

**Useful experience:** A buying brief plus two or three contenders, explicit compromises and the one question the community can help resolve. A native comparison/voting experience is a later possibility, not required to learn whether the brief helps.

**How buyers could find it:** Relevant existing threads and moderator-approved recurring formats or pilots. A founder-owned subreddit is a sandbox, not assumed distribution.

**Bridge to the dealer:** Once the buyer favours a model or listing: confirm equipment, obtain an itemised price, ask about records/inspection, or arrange a fit/test drive.

**Small first exploration:** Prepare one complete example and seek permission for a relevant use; do not send outreach or publish within this research session.

**Movement that would matter:** A real buyer supplies constraints or contenders, uses the brief, then intentionally continues to a relevant Edmunds listing/dealer contact.

**Practical boundary:** Native app approval, moderator installation and permission for outbound journeys are separate gates. Helpful participation and native app deployment are distinct routes.

### 2. Google and Bing Search; related search surfaces

**Natural behaviour:** Explicit questions about two finalists, a constraint, model year/trim, price or what to ask before buying.

**Useful experience:** A focused comparison or decision page with a useful interactive continuation, e.g. older hybrid versus newer petrol within a fixed budget. Resolve the general question; then identify the buyer's location, candidate vehicles and specific next check.

**How buyers could find it:** Useful public pages found through queries and legitimate links. Google AI Overviews/AI Mode and Bing/Copilot citations are additional possible exposure, not separate guaranteed funnels. Yahoo and DuckDuckGo belong in the search review; exposure on each must be observed independently.

**Bridge to the dealer:** Request the actual vehicle's full price, confirm required equipment or compare test drives. An estimate is not a dealer quote.

**Small first exploration:** Adapt one existing relevant asset to one concrete decision rather than create a large generic article batch.

**Movement that would matter:** A relevant query yields a real visit, buyer decision activity and a qualified affiliate continuation; separately observe an approved action.

**Practical boundary:** Existing small traffic is evidence of access, not proof of viability. Ranking/citations do not guarantee clicks. No search volumes or conversion forecasts were collected in this exercise.

### 3. Facebook Groups

**Natural behaviour:** A practical recommendation from people sharing a life situation: parenting, relocation, outdoor activities or a vehicle interest.

**Useful experience:** A helpful case such as 'SUV versus minivan for this family' with an accessible trade-off card and a brief members can discuss with their household.

**How buyers could find it:** Existing relevant groups through permitted helpful participation or an administrator-approved format. A new brand Page is an identity surface, not assumed organic reach.

**Bridge to the dealer:** Try the child seats, check accessibility/cargo fit, confirm a necessary feature, or arrange a test drive for a shortlisted local vehicle.

**Small first exploration:** Identify one community with an actual relevant decision and confirm what useful participation it permits before preparing a pilot.

**Movement that would matter:** Members present real buying situations and use the advice to shortlist and proceed.

**Practical boundary:** Group rules and administrator permission govern participation; no automated unsolicited posts/DMs. Illustrative life situations are not researched demand estimates.

### 4. YouTube

**Natural behaviour:** Seeing comparisons, demonstrations, trim differences and checks before a purchase.

**Useful experience:** A focused narrated decision case or screen demonstration: 'The cheaper listing may not be the cheaper purchase' or 'What to verify before buying a tow vehicle'. Use licensed/source-supported material; do not pretend a screen review is a hands-on road test.

**How buyers could find it:** Searchable focused videos plus relevant channel discovery; short clips can introduce the same question.

**Bridge to the dealer:** Confirm the exact feature/package, request a written total price, or book a test drive to resolve what the video cannot establish about that car.

**Small first exploration:** One useful low-cost narrated comparison linked to its matching decision page, without assuming professional filming costs.

**Movement that would matter:** A viewer follows to the matching resource, personalises a shortlist and proceeds to a dealer journey.

**Practical boundary:** Ordinary Shorts description/comment URLs are not clickable. Long-form external link eligibility requires advanced features; profile/related-video routes differ. Video views alone are not buying intent.

### 5. Specialist forums and enthusiast communities

**Natural behaviour:** Detailed model-year, trim, equipment and use-case advice.

**Useful experience:** An evidence-backed model/year/package buying sheet with checks for exact suitability, rather than broad AI recommendations.

**How buyers could find it:** Permitted expert contributions, useful referenced resources or a willing community partner. Named car forums require separate community checks; Edmunds-hosted forums are not treated as an affiliate-placement shortcut.

**Bridge to the dealer:** Ask for a build sheet, confirm a package/feature, request history/maintenance documents or arrange an inspection.

**Small first exploration:** Choose one question we can answer accurately and investigate access to the relevant community.

**Movement that would matter:** A prospective buyer uses the configuration advice to evaluate actual vehicles and continues to a suitable listing.

**Practical boundary:** Community access and verified specifications are necessary. Critical towing/fit claims must be exact to the vehicle; do not infer unknown equipment.

### 6. Instagram

**Natural behaviour:** Visual explanations, concise case stories and sending a comparison to another person.

**Useful experience:** A carousel comparing two buyer choices, a Reel explaining one compromise, and an accompanying personalised brief.

**How buyers could find it:** Useful visual cases, permitted creator collaborations and audience sharing; a new account's reach is unproven.

**Bridge to the dealer:** Move from the appealing model to the actual local car: feature verification, full price and fit/test drive.

**Small first exploration:** Reuse one evidence-backed case in a visual format with a matching substantive destination.

**Movement that would matter:** Shares/saves followed by attributable destination use and buying activity; saves are only an intermediate signal.

**Practical boundary:** Confirm available outbound-link surfaces on the actual account. Do not assume comments or feed captions supply a working clickable funnel.

### 7. TikTok

**Natural behaviour:** A concise human explanation that gives the viewer a reason to reconsider a car choice.

**Useful experience:** A recurring 'same budget, different compromise' story: a cheaper high-mileage car versus a dearer lower-mileage option, with clear evidence limits.

**How buyers could find it:** Native short video and permitted collaborations; recommendations/virality not assumed.

**Bridge to the dealer:** Personalise the example, shortlist actual vehicles and ask for written price/equipment details.

**Small first exploration:** Reuse a good case to test whether viewers ask genuine buying questions; verify website-link eligibility before treating it as an outbound channel.

**Movement that would matter:** Relevant buyer questions and attributable website/Edmunds continuation rather than views.

**Practical boundary:** Indexed official guidance describes website-link requirements of at least 1,000 followers or a Registered Business Account. Actual eligibility needs checking; the direct help-page fetch was blocked.

### 8. Pinterest

**Natural behaviour:** Saveable comparison graphics, checklists and decision plans.

**Useful experience:** A visual family-car shortlist or dealer-question checklist that leads to the matching complete guide/decision tool.

**How buyers could find it:** Relevant Pins and boards linked to substantive matching pages; search/save behaviour is a fit hypothesis, not a measured automotive demand result.

**Bridge to the dealer:** When planning becomes choosing: find current local options and take the shortlist/checklist to a price request or visit.

**Small first exploration:** Turn an existing useful checklist into a small set of accurate matching Pins.

**Movement that would matter:** Outbound visits that progress into actual vehicle selection; saves alone are not success.

**Practical boundary:** Pinterest says Pin/destination alignment and page quality affect whether Visit site is prominent. Evergreen guidance fits better than presenting stale listings as current stock.

### 9. ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and other AI assistants

**Natural behaviour:** Explain needs, refine a shortlist and ask follow-up questions.

**Useful experience:** Use existing CarClever host experiences for people reached elsewhere. Web citations to useful GetCarWise resources are a distinct discovery mechanism from installed/connected app invocation.

**How buyers could find it:** Deliberate user selection/direct listing links, existing connected users, or observed web-source citations. Perplexity and other assistant inclusion are possibilities, not claims of an available installed integration.

**Bridge to the dealer:** Identify a preferred vehicle and make an informed request for its price, equipment or appointment.

**Small first exploration:** Reuse current working surfaces with a precise decision promise rather than build another generic host app.

**Movement that would matter:** Real invoked sessions progress to suitable listings and attributable approved actions.

**Practical boundary:** OpenAI enhanced distribution is selective. Claude suggestions concern connected apps. Citation, installation, invocation, outbound click and lead are separate events.

### 10. Quora

**Natural behaviour:** Complete answers to specific comparison and buying questions.

**Useful experience:** A self-contained researched explanation with affiliation disclosed; reference a genuinely helpful owned resource only where allowed and directly relevant.

**How buyers could find it:** Existing questions and readers; no broad automated answer programme.

**Bridge to the dealer:** Indirect: a buyer voluntarily uses an allowed resource to personalise their choice, then selects an appropriate Edmunds path.

**Small first exploration:** Review one relevant question for whether a substantive answer adds value.

**Movement that would matter:** Relevant referred users and downstream action, not answer views.

**Practical boundary:** Quora prohibits affiliate links in answers and unhelpful self-promotion. An owned-site link is not a loophole for these restrictions. Hence low priority for this affiliate-centred brief.

### 11. X, Threads and Bluesky

**Natural behaviour:** A concise opinion/comparison that starts a useful conversation.

**Useful experience:** Two cars, one budget, one key trade-off; invite substantive reasoning and provide a matching detailed resource where permitted.

**How buyers could find it:** Existing relevant conversations and relationships; no assumed audience from creating profiles.

**Bridge to the dealer:** Interested buyers follow the comparison into a real shortlist and a price or feature request.

**Small first exploration:** Repurpose a strong case rather than maintain an independent daily content operation.

**Movement that would matter:** Actual buyer conversation and useful outbound progression.

**Practical boundary:** General discussion can attract enthusiasts rather than current buyers. Account-specific link features and reach were not audited.

### 12. LinkedIn

**Natural behaviour:** Professional discussions and relationships with organisations serving consumers.

**Useful experience:** Show a credible case to an educator, publisher, independent inspector or another potential distribution partner; the consumer then uses the helpful resource.

**How buyers could find it:** Relevant professional connections or permitted partner research/outreach, separately authorised.

**Bridge to the dealer:** The partner supplies buyer access; the buyer still deliberately follows a suitable Edmunds/dealer journey.

**Small first exploration:** Identify a plausible partner use case; do not launch a consumer newsletter simply because the platform supports it.

**Movement that would matter:** A willing partner with real buyer access and source-identifiable downstream use.

**Practical boundary:** Lower direct-consumer fit for this brief. A newsletter feature does not supply an audience or prove a viable partnership.


## Other surfaces and shared support

- **Owned GetCarWise website:** the common substantive destination, not an additional acquisition source by itself. Provide the matching answer and let the buyer continue without restarting.
- **Email/newsletters:** possible opted-in follow-up and potential publisher distribution. A small existing buyer list or willing publisher would change priority; neither is established here. Avoid generic newsletters to episodic buyers with no immediate useful purpose.
- **WhatsApp, Messenger, iMessage and email sharing:** a portable shortlist/decision brief supports household collaboration. User-initiated sharing is a mechanism, not permission for unsolicited outreach.
- **Discord and private communities:** analogous to forums when an existing relevant community welcomes help. Could move much higher with a willing community; starting an empty server is not a distribution solution.
- **Automotive marketplaces:** useful for understanding buyer behaviour and resolving stock choices. Do not assume permission to insert affiliate links into competing marketplace discussions/listings. Edmunds is the intended continuation, not an extra audience we automatically control.
- **Google Maps/Apple Maps:** users looking up dealers are often already near contact, but GetCarWise is not a dealership. No strategy based on impersonating a dealer or inventing a local presence. Investigate only a legitimate applicable business/partner route.
- **Native mobile app stores:** a possible delivery surface later; installation alone does not solve access. No new mobile build proposed under the present learning brief.
- **Snapchat/Twitch and other broad entertainment surfaces:** currently lower priority than the ranked routes because this session has not identified a concrete buyer-access advantage. Revisit only with a specific audience or collaborator.
- **Paid Search/social/video:** separate acquisition methods, not organic platform fit. Keep media uncommitted under the owner's cap. A future small paid experiment would need a selected journey, measurable outcome and a separately chosen spending amount.

## Combining angles without opening twelve projects

One evidence-backed buying case can become a community brief, a focused website decision page, a narrated explanation and a visual comparison. Adapt to the native behaviour instead of pasting identical promotional content everywhere.

Illustrative case: an older hybrid versus a newer petrol SUV at a similar purchase budget. Reddit asks for owner experience; Facebook frames the household compromise; Search answers the explicit model/budget question; YouTube shows the comparison; Instagram/Pinterest make a clear saveable card. The buyer then identifies actual Edmunds candidates and asks for full prices or confirms required equipment. No hypothetical model verdict or live availability is asserted.

The first pair to develop in discussion is Reddit/community decision support plus a matching owned decision resource accessible through Search. Facebook and specialist communities are alternatives if access is more credible. Video and visual surfaces can amplify the same useful work rather than require separate products. This is a proposed learning sequence, not approval to post, build or spend.

## What counts as movement

Track stages separately: relevant exposure, real non-test buyer participation, useful decision completion, suitable tracked Edmunds continuation, submitted eligible action, approved action/revenue. Qualitative feedback can explain failure between stages.

Views, saves, citations, moderator permission and working software are useful intermediate signals; none alone establishes commercial success. No arbitrary conversion-rate or traffic threshold is invented here. A willingness to continue spending above the owner's ceiling remains his decision based on actual progress.
