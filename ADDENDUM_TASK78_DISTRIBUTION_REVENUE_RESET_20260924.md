## Decision in one page

**Stop treating Reddit or a new website module as the traffic engine.** The archived Reddit tracker records 81 items, 6,715 real views, six brand mentions and 13 net votes as of Aug 9. A separate GetCarWiseAdvisor account is recorded as banned. These records do not establish that the current account is shadowbanned, nor do they show a meaningful buyer/approved-lead channel. Do not relaunch a Reddit posting cadence.

**Primary acquisition bet:** earn placements from *existing US buyer audiences*—small automotive/personal-finance newsletters, independent buying educators, and consumer-oriented publishers—with a distinctive, free second-opinion workflow they can genuinely recommend. The channel is the partner's audience and link, not the Decision Center page. Start with a researched shortlist and tailored pitches only after approval; do not promise placements. Offer the current truthful tool and a short example, then point to the live app directly or a genuinely useful owned route according to audience intent. Every partner gets a distinct source marker, subject to actual link and consent checks. No commission sharing or subaffiliate assumption without the current partner contract.

**Monetization bet:** retain Edmunds New/Used US$10 approved lead as the verified baseline, but investigate a complementary paid buyer action at the listing-check stage. AutoCheck publicly advertises a vehicle-history-report affiliate program; a pre-purchase-inspection referral or finance offer is only a candidate until the provider's actual publisher eligibility, payout, attribution, consent and fit are verified. Do not describe a free VIN identity/buyer check as a purchased history report. Show independent next steps as choices, not a forced sequence. The Impact marketplace can be searched for approved, relevant programs; availability and terms are account-specific.

**Paid acquisition:** Google Search reaches query intent directly, but the current payout makes it difficult to buy profitably. Maximum break-even CPC before other costs is US$10 × approved-leads-per-paid-click: 2%=US$0.20, 5%=US$0.50, 10%=US$1.00. None of these approval rates is observed. Do not launch Google, Meta or Reddit ads from the current evidence. A future exact-intent Search test needs a current Edmunds/Impact SEM-terms check (the archived CJ terms restrict brand/category bidding and ad copy), Keyword Planner forecast, verified attribution, improved expected revenue per buyer or conservative margin, an André-approved loss cap and a hard stop. Social ads have weaker immediate buying intent and no reason to lead.
