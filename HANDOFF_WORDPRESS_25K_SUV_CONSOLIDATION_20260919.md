## H2 — What matters most at a $25,000 used-SUV budget?

A hard budget changes how you should compare vehicles.

Do not assume the newest model year is automatically the best buy. A lower-mileage older vehicle with strong records can be a better purchase than a newer example with accident history, deferred maintenance or a poor trim/value combination.

4. **Trim/equipment**

This guide uses representative 2021–2022 specifications to explain the differences between the models. Your exact used vehicle may differ by year, trim and drivetrain.

## H2 — Honda CR-V: best all-round practicality

The CR-V is the safest starting point if you do not have a highly specialized requirement.

For 2021, common gas trims combine:

- five-seat packaging;
- **39.2 cu ft** of cargo room behind the rear seats;
- available AWD;
- strong fuel economy for the class.

The CR-V's advantage is not one spectacular number. It is the lack of a major compromise.

### Why buy

- strong passenger/cargo packaging;
- efficient gas powertrain;
- widely available FWD and AWD configurations;
- easy benchmark against every other compact SUV.

### Watch for

Do not assume every CR-V is equivalent.

Check:

- gas vs. hybrid;
- FWD vs. AWD;
- trim;
- accident/title history;
- maintenance;
- exact recall status.

A CR-V Hybrid has different cargo/fuel-economy characteristics from the gas model, so do not transfer gas-model specifications to the hybrid.

---

## H2 — Toyota RAV4: best for resale demand and powertrain choice

The RAV4 is the other obvious benchmark.

The 2021 gas model offers roughly **37.6 cu ft** of cargo room, available AWD and strong fuel economy. The same generation also gives buyers gas, hybrid and—in higher-budget cases—Prime PHEV alternatives.

At $25,000, the key is to avoid paying for reputation alone.

### Why buy

- broad buyer demand;
- available AWD;
- gas and hybrid choices in the used market;
- practical cargo space.

### Watch for

Compare the exact RAV4 against a similar CR-V, CX-5 or Forester.

A strong badge does not make:

- high mileage;
- weak service history;
- accident damage;
- an inflated asking price

automatically acceptable.

---

## H2 — Mazda CX-5: best if cabin quality and driving feel matter

The CX-5 gives this group a different personality.

For 2021, the standard 2.5-liter version is rated around:

- **25/31 MPG** with FWD;
- **24/30 MPG** with AWD;
- **30.9 cu ft** of cargo space behind the rear seats.

That cargo figure is smaller than CR-V, RAV4, Rogue and the redesigned Tucson.

### Why buy

Choose the CX-5 when you are willing to trade some maximum cargo volume for:

- a more driver-focused feel;
- a cabin that tends to feel less utilitarian;
- conventional compact-SUV dimensions.

### Watch for

Compare standard-engine and turbo versions separately.

Turbo trims have different performance, fuel economy and used-market pricing. Do not treat the cheapest CX-5 and a loaded/turbo CX-5 as equivalent vehicles.

---

## H2 — Subaru Forester: best if AWD is non-negotiable

The Forester is the clearest choice here when standard AWD and visibility are core priorities.

For 2021:

- Subaru Symmetrical AWD is standard;
- EPA rating is **26 city / 33 highway MPG**;
- cargo volume is about **31.1 cu ft without the moonroof** and **28.9 cu ft with it**;
- maximum cargo volume can exceed 69 cu ft depending on configuration.

### Why buy

- standard AWD;
- practical seating/cabin shape;
- strong outward visibility;
- useful ground clearance.

### Watch for

The cargo specification varies with moonroof/equipment.

More importantly, verify maintenance history and the exact condition of the AWD vehicle you are buying rather than assuming the drivetrain badge guarantees a good example.

---

## H2 — Nissan Rogue: best for space and value-shopping flexibility

The 2021 Rogue redesign created a genuinely roomy compact SUV.

Official 2021 Nissan specifications list:

- **36.5 cu ft** of cargo room without the moonroof;
- FWD or AWD;
- up to **27/35 MPG** on the FWD S;
- **26/33 MPG** on the AWD S.

### Why buy

Rogue can make sense when you want to maximize:

- cabin/cargo usefulness;
- model-year recency;
- choice within a fixed budget.

### Watch for

Do not choose Rogue purely because one listing is cheaper.

Compare:

- trim;
- drivetrain;
- service history;
- mileage;
- recall status;
- price against similar local vehicles.

Use the extra shopping flexibility to reject weak examples.

---

## H2 — Hyundai Tucson: best newer-generation space/technology value

The 2022 Tucson redesign is worth separating from the older 2021 Tucson.

Hyundai increased official cargo volume from **31.0 cu ft in 2021 to 38.7 cu ft for 2022**, along with a larger passenger cabin.

The 2022 gas powertrain uses a 2.5-liter engine, with FWD or available HTRAC AWD.

### Why buy

A 2022-era Tucson is worth cross-shopping if your $25,000 budget can buy:

- a newer-generation cabin;
- strong cargo space;
- modern safety/infotainment equipment.

### Warranty note

Do not assume Hyundai's new-car **10-year/100,000-mile powertrain warranty** transfers unchanged to an ordinary used buyer.

Hyundai states that the full 10/100 powertrain term is an **original-owner benefit**. Ordinary subsequent owners receive powertrain coverage under the **5-year/60,000-mile** New Vehicle Limited Warranty.

Hyundai Certified Used Vehicles can carry separate certified powertrain coverage, so verify whether the specific vehicle is ordinary used or Hyundai Certified.

### Watch for

Model generation matters.

A 2021 Tucson and a 2022 Tucson have materially different space and design. Do not compare them as though they are the same vehicle with a one-year age difference.

## H2 — Frequently asked questions

### What is the best used compact SUV under $25,000?

There is no universal winner. CR-V is the strongest all-round starting point, RAV4 is a strong resale/powertrain-choice benchmark, Forester is the clearest standard-AWD choice, CX-5 favors cabin/driving feel, Rogue can offer space/value flexibility, and the 2022 Tucson gives you a newer-generation space/technology option.

### Can I get a reliable compact SUV for $25,000?

Yes, but the budget alone does not guarantee a strong vehicle. Compare mileage, maintenance, accident/title history, drivetrain, recalls and the exact asking price before deciding.

### Should I buy the newest SUV I can find under $25,000?

Not automatically. A newer model with poor history or high mileage can be worse value than an older vehicle with excellent records and condition.

### Which compact SUV under $25,000 is best for AWD?

Subaru Forester makes AWD standard. RAV4, CR-V and CX-5 also offer AWD, but verify the drivetrain on the exact listing.

### Does Hyundai's 10-year/100,000-mile warranty transfer to a used Tucson?

Not in full for an ordinary subsequent owner. Hyundai says the full 10-year/100,000-mile powertrain warranty is an original-owner benefit; subsequent owners generally receive powertrain coverage under the 5-year/60,000-mile New Vehicle Limited Warranty. Hyundai Certified Used Vehicles can have separate certified coverage.
