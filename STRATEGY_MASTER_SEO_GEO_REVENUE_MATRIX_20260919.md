# GetCarWise Master SEO / GEO / Revenue Matrix

**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Owner lane:** ChatGPT — Business/Strategy  
**Status:** Working control matrix. Measured baselines are factual; prioritization scores and treatments are proposed until André approves implementation.

## Purpose

Combine the existing GetCarWise SEO, Google Generative AI, commercial-funnel and evidence-first strategy work into one operating control panel.

This matrix is deliberately **not** a simple ranking of pages. It separates:

1. **Opportunity value** — how strategically valuable a page could be across acquisition, AI visibility and revenue.
2. **Intervention urgency** — whether we should rebuild it now, protect it, fix architecture first, or leave it as a control.

That distinction prevents us from damaging pages that already work well in GEO or conventional Search merely because their business value is high.

## Governing strategy

The commercial hierarchy remains:

> **Discovery → trusted GetCarWise / CarClever decision support → relevant New / Used / Trade-in next action → Edmunds lead → revenue / learning loop**

SEO and GEO are acquisition systems, not the ultimate objective.

The current organic marketing allocation remains:

- **60% shared SEO + GEO foundation**
- **25% SEO-specific demand capture**
- **15% GEO-specific discovery / brand influence**

The page-priority weighting for revenue-oriented work is:

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Revenue / lead-intent fit | 30% |
| SEO opportunity | 20% |
| GEO / AI opportunity | 15% |
| Commercial shopper intent / decision proximity | 15% |
| Cross-surface authority / internal-link value | 10% |
| Proprietary evidence / data advantage | 5% |
| Effort / risk / readiness | 5% |

Scores below are a **prioritization index, not a performance rating**. Each dimension is scored 1–5 and weighted to a 100-point index.

## Measurement cautions

- Google Search impressions and Google Generative AI impressions are different surfaces. Do not divide one by the other as a share calculation.
- GSC page average position blends all queries/devices/contexts. It can differ materially from a tracked specific keyword. Example: the $30k page has a GSC page average around 26 while Ubersuggest previously showed the specific `best suv under 30000` term around position 13.
- The GSC query export is incomplete because of privacy/anonymization. Page-level totals are the primary baseline.
- The $25k SEO and GEO signals currently point to **different URLs**; do not combine them as though they are one page.

# 2. Why the top four are the core learning set

## A. SUV under $30k — combined search experiment

This is the best combined treatment because:

- the main keyword has previously been observed externally around position 13 even though the GSC page-wide average is deeper;
- the page already has substantial content, so generic word-count expansion is not the answer;
- only 3 Google Generative AI impressions were recorded;
- it naturally supports both New and Used lead paths;
- GetCarWise can add something competitors cannot easily duplicate: current market evidence plus a transparent new-vs-used decision framework.

**Treatment:** freshness + current market snapshot + transparent methodology + primary-source citations + answer-first structure + stale claim correction. Preserve URL.

**Do not** simultaneously add a new catalog inventory module during the first treatment. Separate content-effect learning from monetization-module learning.

## B. Used PHEV — high demand, weak ranking, almost no GEO

This is the strongest evidence that Google sees query relevance but the page is not competitive enough yet:

- highest measured conventional content-page impressions: 136;
- average position 43.07;
- only 1 AI impression.

**Treatment:** full evidence-led rebuild around the used-PHEV buying decision, current market availability, price/mileage evidence where validated, battery/warranty context from authoritative sources, clear methodology, decision table and buyer watch-outs.

This is our best test of whether an **SEO-heavy / GEO-weak** page can be converted into a cross-surface asset.

## C. SUV under $25k — solve architecture before content

The conventional Search URL and the Generative AI URL are not the same:

- Search: `/best-compact-suv-under-25000/` — 122 impressions.
- AI: `/best-compact-suv-under-25k-comparison/` — 4 AI impressions.

This is not proof of cannibalization, but it is enough to require an intent/canonical audit before investing in a major rewrite.

**Treatment order:** map both pages → establish distinct user jobs or consolidate → only then apply the evidence-first page standard.

## D. 3-row SUV under $50k — preserve the GEO winner

This page is the most important comparator:

- 41 conventional impressions;
- average position 19.71;
- **78 Generative AI impressions**, the highest measured site page.

The page's structured comparison / “best for” / specs / why-buy / watch-for / table / FAQ pattern is highly extractable, but prior fact-checking also found at least one warranty nuance requiring QA.

**Treatment:** protect its answer architecture, correct factual/source issues, add freshness/reviewer/methodology signals only where needed, and avoid a heavy rewrite while it serves as our GEO benchmark.

# 3. Treatment classes for the wider site

## Class 1 — BUILD / REBUILD

Use when there is proven demand but weak ranking and weak GEO.

Current examples:
- Used PHEV
- later Used EV
- later Hybrid SUV under $20k

Pattern:
- answer the decision first;
- add original/current evidence;
- add methodology;
- use primary sources;
- expose tradeoffs and watch-outs;
- provide a natural next action only after the independent answer.

## Class 2 — OPTIMIZE / CONTROLLED TREATMENT

Use when a page is already close enough to page one that a targeted change can be measured.

Current examples:
- SUV under $30k
- Midsize sedan under $40k
- CarClever-vs-web-search article

Pattern:
- preserve URL and topic;
- avoid full rewrites;
- improve intent match, evidence, snippets, freshness and internal relationships;
- measure 14/28/56-day change.

## Class 3 — PROTECT

Use where the page is already performing strongly on one or both surfaces.

Current examples:
- 3-row SUV under $50k
- Tools hub
- Try CarClever
- Homepage

Pattern:
- factual/freshness QA;
- improve trust/entity clarity;
- descriptive internal links;
- do not add clutter or rewrite simply to chase another metric.

## Class 4 — ARCHITECTURE FIRST

Use where two URLs may split closely related intent.

Current example:
- $25k SUV cluster

Pattern:
- map intent;
- compare queries/backlinks/internal links/canonical/index state;
- consolidate or differentiate;
- then optimize one clear user job per URL.

## Class 5 — CONTROL

Leave selected pages materially unchanged while major treatments run.

Initial controls:
- Hybrid SUV under $20k
- Used EV
- F-150 vs Silverado towing

These are not abandoned. They help prevent us from attributing site-wide or algorithmic movement to our treatment pages incorrectly.

# 4. Revenue-aware page design

The lead path should follow the user's decision, not distort it.

## Used / New buying pages

Preferred hierarchy:

1. independent GetCarWise answer and evidence;
2. decision / comparison;
3. contextual next action;
4. where appropriate, current Edmunds inventory or a specific Edmunds destination;
5. lead completion occurs on Edmunds.

Current Impact facts relevant to the matrix:

- Used lead: **$10**
- New lead: **$10**
- Trade-in lead: **$3.50**
- 30-day attribution window
- Product Catalog API **cannot search by VIN**
- Catalog can support general model/body-style/dealer inventory discovery and returns tracked result links
- catalog coverage is a subset of the broader market, so it must not drive the independent editorial recommendation

## Experiment discipline

For the first SEO/GEO treatment round:

- migrate affiliate tracking when operationally necessary;
- **do not introduce major new visual monetization modules on the same date as a content treatment**;
- record the exact tracking-migration date separately;
- test catalog-backed “current inventory” later as a second experiment on one high-intent page;
- measure lead yield / revenue per qualified visitor, not raw affiliate clicks alone.

# 6. Measurement cadence

For every treatment:

### T0 — pre-change
Use the existing 28-day baseline plus the exact deployment date.

### T+14 days — early directional read
Look for indexing/coverage problems, query mix changes and obvious negative movement. Do not declare success/failure from noise.

### T+28 days — primary comparison
Compare against a matched 28-day pre-period:
- Search impressions/clicks/CTR/position;
- Generative AI impressions;
- exact query movement where available;
- branded/direct or tool engagement where available;
- Impact lead metrics if the page has a monetization path.

### T+56 days — confirmation
Confirm whether the movement persisted, broadened to related queries, and created business value.

# 7. Decision rules

1. **Accuracy and trust outrank monetization.**
2. **One clear user job per URL.**
3. **Original/current evidence outranks generic word count.**
4. **Protect pages that already work; do not optimize them into regression.**
5. **SEO and GEO are measured separately but planned together.**
6. **Revenue fit breaks ties between otherwise comparable opportunities.**
7. **Do not let Edmunds inventory availability determine GetCarWise's independent recommendation.**
8. **Do not mass-generate affiliate inventory pages.**
9. **Separate content-treatment dates from monetization-UI experiments.**
10. **Scale only what the 28–56 day evidence supports.**
