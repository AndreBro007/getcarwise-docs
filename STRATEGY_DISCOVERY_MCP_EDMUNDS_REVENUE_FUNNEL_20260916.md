# GetCarWise Discovery → MCP → Edmunds Revenue Funnel Strategy

**Date:** 2026-09-16  
**Owner lane:** ChatGPT — Business/Strategy  
**Status:** **Proposed — pending André approval before implementation**

## Executive thesis

The CJ → Impact migration changes the strategic hierarchy.

SEO and GEO should no longer be treated as the top-level objective. They are **discovery channels feeding a larger commercial system**:

> **Discover GetCarWise → Trust/evaluate with GetCarWise or CarClever → take the appropriate next action → complete one of three Edmunds lead events.**

The three current revenue events are the commercial north star:

1. **Used Vehicle Lead — $10**
2. **New Vehicle Lead — $10**
3. **Trade-in Offers Lead — $3.50**

Impact's 30-day referral window improves the economics of a long-consideration automotive funnel. A visitor does not need to convert in the same session for the initial qualified handoff to retain potential value.

This does **not** mean every page or app interaction should contain an affiliate CTA. The correct principle is:

> Every acquisition and product experience should either create qualified demand, deepen trust/decision confidence, or move an already-qualified shopper to the most relevant next action.

Raw traffic, AI impressions, app invocations and outbound clicks are intermediate metrics. The final commercial measures are valid new-car, used-car and trade-in leads and the revenue/quality they generate.

# 1. The unified funnel

## Stage A — Discovery

Potential entry surfaces:

- Google organic Search;
- Google AI Overviews / AI Mode;
- ChatGPT app/plugin discovery and conversational app suggestions;
- Claude connector/MCP discovery and conversational suggestions;
- Reddit/community participation;
- referral/backlink traffic;
- direct/branded search;
- future PR/social/email/paid experiments.

Primary job: get GetCarWise/CarClever considered when a consumer has an automotive research, comparison or shopping need.

## Stage B — Evaluation / decision support

Potential experiences:

- GetCarWise editorial/evidence pages;
- Tools hub;
- CarClever website paths;
- CarClever inside ChatGPT;
- CarClever inside Claude;
- deal evaluation / comparison / affordability / risk functions where available.

Primary job: reduce uncertainty and increase confidence in the shopper's next step.

## Stage C — Commercial next action

Three lead destinations:

- **Used:** browse / inspect a specific or comparable used vehicle → Edmunds used-vehicle lead.
- **New:** research current new vehicle / inventory / incentives → Edmunds new-vehicle lead.
- **Trade-in:** value / sell / replace current vehicle → Edmunds trade-in lead.

Primary job: create the cleanest, most contextually relevant handoff to Edmunds without disrupting trust or misleading the user.

## Stage D — Revenue / learning

Measure:

- qualified outbound clicks;
- lead type;
- valid lead completions;
- revenue;
- EPC / revenue per qualified visitor;
- landing page / source / device / campaign;
- time lag to conversion where Impact reporting permits.

Feed results back into page, CTA, app, content and distribution prioritisation.

# 2. How the MCP apps fit into GEO

The ChatGPT and Claude apps create an additional discovery surface that is **related to GEO but not the same thing**.

## Web GEO

Web GEO means GetCarWise pages/brand are retrieved, cited, linked or used in AI-generated answers.

It can create:

- brand familiarity;
- branded searches;
- visits to GetCarWise;
- awareness that CarClever exists;
- a reason to install/connect/use the app later.

## Platform app discovery

Inside ChatGPT or Claude, app/tool selection depends on the host platform's own discovery and tool-routing systems rather than Google ranking.

Relevant factors include:

- whether the connected app/tool actually matches the user's current intent;
- accurate, narrow and non-manipulative tool descriptions;
- app directory/listing clarity;
- product reliability and usefulness;
- user usage/satisfaction signals where the platform considers them;
- platform policy and distribution eligibility.

Therefore:

> Strong SEO/GEO can increase awareness and demand for CarClever, but a high-ranking GetCarWise webpage does not automatically cause ChatGPT or Claude to invoke the MCP tool.

Conversely, successful MCP usage can create branded searches, direct visits, sharing and awareness that later benefits the website.

The correct model is a **flywheel**, not a direct ranking transfer.

# 3. The “millions of listings” signal

The prior OpenAI submission copy includes language describing CarClever as searching **over 3.4 million active U.S. dealer listings**. Later project records indicate the underlying corpus count may have moved materially higher and the preferred public wording evolved toward **“several million active U.S. listings”** pending live verification.

Strategic implication:

- The fact that an AI platform can describe/repeat this capability is evidence that product metadata and public factual positioning can propagate into AI-mediated discovery.
- It is **not** evidence that the website's SEO rank controls MCP routing.
- Corpus-count claims must remain dynamically or periodically verified; do not hard-code stale exact figures across website/app/marketing surfaces.

This strengthens the case for the proposed **Capability & Data Provenance Register**: one factual source of truth for listing corpus, data sources, supported vehicle types, title/history limitations, recall behavior and other public product claims.

# 4. Impact changes page prioritisation

The existing SEO/GEO matrix should gain a **lead-fit dimension**.

A page is no longer valuable only because it can gain Search traffic or AI visibility. It is more valuable when the user's intent naturally maps to a valid revenue action.

## Recommended priority weights

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Revenue / lead-intent fit | **30%** |
| SEO opportunity | **20%** |
| GEO / AI opportunity | **15%** |
| Commercial shopper intent / decision proximity | **15%** |
| Cross-surface authority / internal-link value | **10%** |
| Proprietary evidence/data advantage | **5%** |
| Effort / risk / readiness | **5%** |

This supersedes the earlier page-priority weighting for revenue-oriented work, while the earlier SEO/GEO weighting remains useful for pure visibility projects.

A high-impression page with poor commercial fit may still matter for authority, but it should not automatically outrank a smaller page with strong, natural used/new/trade-in intent.

# 6. Website is currently the safest monetisation surface

The website is the clearest place to combine independent decision support with disclosed Edmunds next actions.

Recommended pattern:

1. Answer the user's question independently.
2. Show current evidence / market data / limitations.
3. Help the user decide what type of vehicle/action fits.
4. Present one contextual **Next Best Action**.
5. Clearly disclose the affiliate relationship.
6. Qualify commercial links appropriately (`rel="sponsored"` or equivalent implementation).
7. Track intent and destination cleanly.

Do not let affiliate economics distort the editorial answer. If a user's best next action is not an Edmunds lead path, the page should still remain useful and trustworthy.

# 7. Important OpenAI and Anthropic boundary

## OpenAI

Current OpenAI plugin/app policy permits external commerce for physical goods but emphasizes user intent, accurate app behavior and non-deceptive external handoffs. Plugins must not serve advertisements or primarily function as advertising vehicles.

Current guidance centers external purchase completion on the developer's own domain. Therefore **direct app-side Edmunds affiliate monetisation should not be assumed compliant without explicit policy confirmation**.

Proposed safe default until confirmed:

- CarClever remains a neutral user-utility product inside ChatGPT.
- When a user genuinely needs a richer/transactional continuation, route only in a way permitted by OpenAI policy.
- Prefer the GetCarWise-owned web experience as the monetised continuation layer rather than inserting sponsored Edmunds advertising into the plugin.
- Do not manipulate tool descriptions or prompts to increase calls for affiliate reasons.

## Anthropic

Anthropic's current software-directory policy explicitly disallows software that serves advertisements, sponsored content or paid product placements, or primarily functions as an advertising/promotional vehicle. Claude also states connected apps are suggested based on usefulness/context rather than paid placement.

Therefore:

> **Do not place affiliate advertising or sponsored Edmunds promotions inside the Claude-listed MCP unless Anthropic explicitly confirms a compliant mechanism.**

The Claude app should remain utility-first. Any web continuation should exist because it serves the user's requested task, not as a disguised monetisation redirect.

This policy boundary makes the website even more important as the current monetisation layer.

# 8. Impact Publisher Tag and assets

## Publisher Tag

Potential website benefits:

- reduce manual affiliate wrapping;
- preserve ordinary Edmunds destination URLs in editorial workflows;
- centralise Impact tracking;
- add impression-level tracking.

Before adoption, Claude should verify in a non-production test:

- generated destination correctness;
- behavior with VIN/deep links;
- whether tracking parameters / subIDs can preserve page and intent attribution;
- whether link attributes such as `rel="sponsored"` remain correct;
- page-performance impact;
- consent/privacy implications;
- compatibility with caching / WordPress / existing analytics;
- failure behavior if the Impact script is blocked.

No production rollout should occur merely because the tag is convenient.

## Banner assets / ads

Treat the 9 Impact banners as **test inventory**, not as the strategy.

Contextual next-action links are likely to align better with GetCarWise's trust positioning and high-intent funnel than generic banners.

Recommended rule:

- no site-wide banner rollout;
- do not put banners inside ChatGPT/Claude apps;
- if tested on the website, use a controlled placement against a contextual CTA and measure lead yield, engagement and page UX;
- stop if banners reduce trust or distract from the decision task.

# 9. Edmunds Product Feed could connect SEO/GEO and revenue — but only after rights/schema review

The Impact account exposes an Edmunds catalog reported at approximately **1.33 million products/listings**, updated at least daily.

This could become strategically important in four ways:

1. **Freshness validation** — confirm whether vehicles/models referenced in GetCarWise content have meaningful current Edmunds availability.
2. **Market evidence** — potentially contribute listing counts, price/mileage distributions or availability trends to proprietary research.
3. **Lead routing** — identify the most relevant Edmunds destination for a high-intent shopper.
4. **Cross-source validation** — compare selected fields against Auto.dev to improve confidence and detect drift.

However, do **not** use it to mass-generate thin affiliate pages.

Before using feed data in public SEO/GEO content, confirm:

- catalog schema and actual field quality;
- new vs used coverage;
- VIN/destination URL fields;
- update cadence;
- duplicate/dealer coverage;
- rights to publish derived/aggregated statistics;
- restrictions on storing/caching/displaying fields;
- whether the catalog materially overlaps Auto.dev and whether sources are independent.

Until those questions are answered, the feed is an opportunity, not an approved content source.

By **source → landing page/app → intent → CTA → lead type**:

- used lead completions × $10;

- total revenue;

This allows SEO and GEO to be compared on economic value without pretending their impression metrics are equivalent.

# 12. The commercial content strategy becomes “decision → action”

The evidence-first SEO/GEO strategy remains correct, but each high-value page should now explicitly answer:

1. **What decision is this shopper trying to make?**
2. **What proprietary/current evidence can GetCarWise add?**
3. **What is the safest/best next action after the decision?**
4. **Which of the three revenue paths, if any, naturally matches that action?**

Examples:

### SUV under $30k

- Discovery: `best SUV under 30000` + AI multi-constraint recommendations.
- Evidence: current new/used market snapshot.
- Decision: new vs used + best fit.
- Action: context-specific new or used inventory handoff.

### Used PHEV

- Discovery: `best used PHEV`, EV/PHEV comparison prompts.
- Evidence: current used price, mileage, availability, battery/warranty context where verified.
- Decision: which used PHEV fits.
- Action: comparable used inventory.

### Trade-in content

- Discovery: valuation/sell/trade-in queries and AI questions.
- Evidence: transparent explanation of valuation/tradeoffs.
- Decision: sell/trade/keep.
- Action: Edmunds trade-in offer only where it genuinely matches the outcome.

# 14. Updated strategic allocation

The prior marketing recommendation of **60% shared SEO+GEO foundation / 25% SEO-specific / 15% GEO-specific** remains valid for acquisition work.

But the governing commercial framework becomes:

> **Acquisition investment is prioritised by expected qualified-lead value, not traffic alone.**

Within the shared 60%, prioritize assets that can do all four:

1. earn organic visibility;
2. be retrieved/cited by AI;
3. demonstrate CarClever/GetCarWise capability and trust;
4. naturally move a qualified shopper toward a relevant new/used/trade-in action.

This is why proprietary market-data assets become more valuable after the Impact migration, not less.

# Decision rules

1. **User trust and factual accuracy outrank affiliate yield.**
2. A page must answer the user's question without requiring an affiliate click.
3. Monetisation should appear at the moment of natural action, not at every impression.
4. One relevant next action usually beats multiple competing affiliate links.
5. SEO/GEO work should be scored partly on lead potential, but not warped into thin affiliate content.
6. MCP apps remain utility products; do not convert them into ad/sponsored surfaces where platform policy prohibits that.
7. Website commercial links must be disclosed and appropriately qualified.
8. Do not mass-publish Edmunds feed pages. Use the feed only after schema/rights validation and where it creates original user value.
9. Measure leads/revenue, not just clicks.
10. Preserve separate platform measurements while connecting them through a common business funnel.

# Bottom line

The Impact migration strengthens, rather than invalidates, the SEO/GEO strategy.

The new governing model is:

> **SEO + GEO + ChatGPT/Claude discovery create qualified demand → GetCarWise/CarClever creates confidence → the website/product routes the user to the right next action → Edmunds monetises new, used or trade-in intent.**

The long-term advantage is a flywheel: original market evidence improves web and AI visibility; visibility grows CarClever adoption; product usage reveals high-value shopper intents; those intents create better contextual lead handoffs; revenue and partner data fund better evidence/content/product experiences.

The three lead events are the commercial north star, but trust and independent decision usefulness remain the mechanism that makes the funnel durable.
