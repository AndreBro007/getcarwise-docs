## A. Executive decision

**Recommendation:** For the next 90 days, make the WordPress site and its decision journey the acquisition and measurement hub. Position GetCarWise as a practical second opinion for a US buyer who has a budget, shortlist or specific listing. Route from useful advice to the live Decision Center, Lite V2 or a clearly labelled partner action. Measure the accepted lead, not the click. Maintain ChatGPT, Claude and Meta Muse submissions as contingent distribution options, then promote them only after approval, discoverability and source-specific outcome are demonstrated. Do not launch the public Impact catalog, broad paid traffic, or a subscription proposition now.

The immediate binding constraint is evidence, not a shortage of ideas: 29 GSC US clicks in the latest reviewed 28-day window, two historical completed leads without reliable source attribution, and an incomplete site→Lite→Impact reconciliation. Sept 30 should be an instrument-and-cost gate. It cannot statistically declare product-market failure from a few days with no leads.

The 90-day objective is a repeatable, auditable journey with one acquisition cluster and one partner-lead path. A useful success condition is **three consecutive weekly reports** that can reconcile source, landing, CTA/tool, tagged outbound and accepted/declined lead by platform and page; then enough qualified events to compare conversion, with no fabricated attribution. Revenue is a consequence measure. Set numeric traffic and lead targets only after the first measured baseline, not from the marketplace submissions or keyword-volume estimates.

**Decision request after review:** approve the sequencing and bounded tests, identify a weekly operating-hours ceiling and maximum experimental cash budget, and appoint the owner for affiliate reporting. Without these inputs, use the conservative no-new-spend path below.

## C. Market, competitors and a defensible wedge

Cox Automotive's 2026 in-market survey reports 63% definite/probable AI use in car shopping (n=1,502), a directional category signal, not a GetCarWise forecast ([Cox](https://www.coxautoinc.com/press-releases/new-cox-automotive-ai-in-auto-retail-tracker/)). The buyer still has high-stakes uncertainty about fit, true budget, condition, dealer availability and next steps. Large sites solve breadth: [Edmunds](https://www.edmunds.com/car-buying/how-to-use-the-edmunds-new-car-inventory-tool.html) provides local inventory/filters and contact, [CarGurus](https://www.cargurus.com/research/car-valuation) rates deals against market value, and [CARFAX](https://support.carfax.com/article/what-is-carfax-used-car-listings/) attaches history to its used listings. A smaller player should win a narrower decision moment, not promise to beat them on inventory depth, valuation or history.

**Chosen wedge:** “A clear second opinion before you contact a dealer.” Practical promise: turn a budget, shortlist or vehicle of interest into a small set of sensible next questions and a transparent route to act. Distinctives can be honest trade-offs, an explicit “what we do not know” line, a VIN-specific entry where supported, and a partner handoff that does not masquerade as independent evidence. Test the promise in copy before expanding functionality.

| Audience/job | Entry point | Useful answer and next step | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Budget-constrained shortlist buyer (“best SUV under $30k/$25k,” PHEV, family three-row $50k) | Existing search guide → Decision Center | 2–3 trade-offs, ownership/fit checklist, Lite V2 where a concrete search can help | 1, because GSC has observed impressions and pages already exist |
| Used-listing evaluator with a VIN | Direct Buyer Check or guide → exact-VIN path | Separate identity/available fields from accident/title/maintenance facts that are **not** supplied; ask for independent inspection/history and compare alternatives | 1, for action intent, but avoid the word “history report” |
| New/Used ready to contact a dealer | Decision Center/Lite V2 → labelled Edmunds partner destination | Explain relevance and referral relationship; confirm the click lands at the expected offer | 1, monetization path |
| Trade-in curiosity | Decision Center Trade-in | Use the $3.50 valid-lead economics and truthful appraisal limitations; secondary route | 2 |
| Early browsing without a budget or shortlist | Broad editorial discovery | Helpful educational answer, not an urgent affiliate pitch | 3 |

The brand voice should be calm, specific and candid. Example: “Three used SUVs near your budget to compare, what each gives up, and what to check before contacting a dealer.” Avoid “AI knows the best car,” “guaranteed deal,” “live local inventory” when unsupported, or implying affiliate placement is an independent ranking.

## E. Revenue model and economics

The present model is qualified partner leads. Track **valid/approved** New and Used at US$10 and Trade-in at US$3.50, subject to the live program terms, reversals and reporting lag. A clicked outbound link is a leading indicator, not revenue. For each source `s`, monthly gross commission = `10 × (approved New_s + approved Used_s) + 3.5 × approved Trade-in_s` in US dollars. Net contribution then deducts paid media and attributable variable costs in the same currency; separately convert to A$ for comparison with billed overhead using an actual dated FX rate.

Illustrative sensitivity, explicitly **not a forecast**: 1,000 qualified sessions × 10% tagged outbound × 5% accepted-lead-per-outbound = 5 leads, US$50 gross if all New/Used. At those assumed rates, US$1,000 gross requires about 20,000 qualified sessions. Even 20% outbound and 10% acceptance require 5,000 sessions for 100 leads. The current 29 organic clicks per 28 days make a large near-term affiliate revenue target implausible without a new, measured distribution engine.

Paid break-even before overhead: maximum CPC = `US$10 × approved-lead-per-paid-click` for New/Used. Five percent means US$0.50, 10% US$1.00; Trade-in is US$0.175/US$0.35. A hypothetical US$5 CPC requires 50% accepted New/Used lead conversion merely to pay media. Do not buy broad paid search now. If qualified direct/publisher traffic later validates conversion, evaluate a capped campaign against **observed** CPC and approval rate with a margin requirement. Do not confuse Ubersuggest keyword CPC estimates with actual clearing prices.

Subscription, dealer SaaS, sponsored placement, data licensing and a marketplace are **adjacent hypotheses**, not a 90-day base case. Each adds sales, policy, fulfillment and trust burden while the lead funnel is unproven. Partner diversification is sensible once current Impact actions reconcile, to reduce one-program risk; compare contracts and user fit before implementation. Never use the private catalog's count as a public commerce promise.

## G. Analytics and decision-grade attribution

Adopt one event dictionary across surfaces: `landing_view` (source/medium/campaign, page, device), `decision_start` (job), `tool_result` (success/empty/error), `partner_cta_view`, `partner_outbound` (offer, placement, page, platform, immutable click key), and `approved_action` (Impact action ID, payout, approval date, rejection/reversal). Do not put VIN, precise location or personal data in analytics identifiers. Use Impact Sub IDs for source/placement/click correlation within its limits; preserve the click parameter through the actual destination and inspect accepted actions rather than assuming a GA4 event is a sale. [Impact Sub ID explanation](https://help.impact.com/en/support/solutions/articles/48001249593-sub-ids-and-shared-ids-explained).

The weekly dashboard should report, by owned organic, direct, referral/community, ChatGPT, Claude, Muse and paid if ever enabled: eligible visits or invocations; result success; CTA view; tagged click; accepted/declined/pending action; commission and reversal; spend; data completeness. Show a denominator for every rate. Use distinct “unknown source” bucket. Where app platforms do not expose impressions, label the top of funnel **unobservable**; do not divide leads by invented exposure. Reconcile GSC clicks to landing sessions with date/time-zone and consent differences noted. The reported GA4/GSC link is not proof of event collection; validate on a consented test journey and an Impact test/report where permitted. Apply UTM tags to owned routes, while preserving affiliate link parameters and privacy.

**Minimum credible funnel gate:** three test journeys per route (WordPress→partner, WordPress→Lite→partner, and each approved app→partner) show distinct source/placement IDs, expected destination and matching event sequence; one actual action correlation or Impact support/report confirmation validates post-click attribution. If a test lead is not allowed, label the accepted-action stage unverified. Owners: André for account access and commercial acceptance; technical owner for event and link validation; editorial owner for page labels. No account changes are authorized by this strategy.

## O. Final recommendation and boundaries

Approve a **measurement-first, owned-search-led, second-opinion strategy** for the next 90 days. Fix attribution and legacy paths, protect the observed content cohorts, improve one decision journey, and test useful distribution in a narrow lane. Keep the three assistant submissions ready but give them no base-case revenue until approved and observed. Preserve the catalog privately and make no speculative inventory or vehicle-history claims. The present US$10 lead economics argue against broad paid search and a product expansion until the partner funnel has an accepted-action baseline.

The recommendation would change if (1) an assistant surface produces sustained verified leads at lower effort than the owned site, (2) qualified outbound traffic consistently fails to produce accepted actions after tracking and partner landing are validated, (3) a catalog test resolves field integrity and attribution, or (4) new program terms materially change payout. André's review should set the capacity/cash envelope and choose whether to proceed with the implementation backlog. No operational action is made by this document.
