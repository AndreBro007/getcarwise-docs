## What changed the decision

1. **The opportunity is a guided decision and handoff, not another inventory marketplace.** Edmunds already offers new/used inventory, local ZIP and radius filters, reviews, deal ratings, finance and trade tools; CarGurus has local market-value/deal ratings; CARFAX pairs each used listing with history. GetCarWise cannot truthfully claim comparable coverage, vehicle history, or deal scoring from V2's present two-tool interface. Position a short, transparent *buyer decision layer* that narrows a choice and explains the next action, then routes to a qualified dealer destination. Sources: [Edmunds tool](https://www.edmunds.com/car-buying/how-to-use-the-edmunds-new-car-inventory-tool.html), [Edmunds about](https://www.edmunds.com/about/), [CarGurus valuation](https://www.cargurus.com/research/car-valuation), [CARFAX listing](https://support.carfax.com/article/what-is-carfax-used-car-listings/).
2. **The market is receptive to AI assistance, but this is not a traffic forecast.** Cox Automotive's Q2 2026 survey of 1,502 in-market consumers says 63% would definitely/probably use AI in car shopping; this cannot be projected to directory impressions or GetCarWise conversions. [Cox primary study](https://www.coxautoinc.com/press-releases/new-cox-automotive-ai-in-auto-retail-tracker/).
3. **Owned search and the decision journey remain the controllable acquisition core.** Google's current guidance says ordinary SEO fundamentals apply to AI Overviews/AI Mode, with no special GEO schema or AI file; indexability, textual substance, internal links and visible-data-consistent structured data matter. Search Console folds AI-feature traffic into Web reporting; isolated “AI referral” claims need a separate, defensible method. [Google AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), [helpful content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content), [AI-generated content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content).
4. **Directory submissions are valuable options, not a forecastable channel.** OpenAI describes app-directory discovery and contextual suggestions but no guaranteed placement. Meta Muse reviews functional/security/legal requirements before directory appearance and does not promise a review SLA. Current ChatGPT, Claude and Muse submissions are pending in the repository; no public discovery or repeatable lead flow can be inferred yet. [OpenAI announcement](https://openai.com/index/developers-can-now-submit-apps-to-chatgpt/), [Muse platform](https://muse.ai/platform).
5. **Commercial links need utility, clarity and platform-specific review.** OpenAI's current published plugin guidelines require purpose-driven behavior, accurate tool descriptions and a valuable experience; plugins must not serve ads or primarily act as an advertising vehicle. Commerce rules and exit links require case-specific interpretation for an affiliate dealer handoff. The policy page uses “plugin” terminology, so check the exact applicable review regime at launch; do not presume all affiliate URLs are permitted or forbidden. Muse's affiliate treatment is not established by its public platform page. Maintain disclosure and distinguish neutral advice from sponsored destinations. [OpenAI guidelines](https://developers.openai.com/plugins/app-guidelines), [Muse platform](https://muse.ai/platform).
6. **Paid search is economically constrained before any campaign.** At US$10 per approved New/Used lead, break-even CPC before other costs is `US$10 × P(approved lead | paid click)`. At 5% it is US$0.50; at 10%, US$1.00. The current historical two leads have no source attribution, and click-to-approved-lead conversion is unmeasured. A high-CPC broad auto keyword campaign cannot be justified by current evidence. This is a unit-economics calculation, not a measured conversion rate.

## Competitive whitespace and limits

| Alternative | Strength observed | Consequence for GetCarWise |
|---|---|---|
| Edmunds | Extensive ZIP/radius inventory, filters, dealer contact, comparisons, pricing and appraisal | Partner/handoff economics can complement, but avoid claiming GetCarWise has the partner's full inventory or tools. |
| CarGurus | Local valuation and deal ratings | Do not revive “Deal Score” without a validated data basis. Offer an explicit checklist or second opinion with limits. |
| CARFAX | Used-listing history and reports | VIN input in GetCarWise is not a vehicle-history report. Show that distinction at the point of use. |
| General AI assistants | Broad reasoning and user relationship | Two precise MCP actions and verified links may be useful; mere submission gives no distribution moat. |

## Demand and measurement readout

Internal GSC's last reviewed 28-day US window (Sep 19) had 29 clicks and about 1,610 impressions. A separate GenAI count of 213 should be retained with its original definition, not combined as incremental visits. Ubersuggest free-tier estimates (Aug: domain authority 9, estimated organic visits 2/month, 35 keywords) are directional and conflict with observed GSC clicks; use GSC for the decision baseline. The keyword list gives limited directional support for budget-SUV, PHEV and three-row-SUV intent, not a reliable market-size forecast. Protect existing $30k, $25k, $40k and three-row $50k observation windows. The prior $30k “collapse” was an exact-query/page-total mix-up.

At this scale, zero leads in a week does not falsify product fit. The next decision-quality result is a reconciled funnel: qualified landing or app session → tool/CTA use → tagged outbound click → Impact accepted lead and payout, by source and destination. Impact's [Sub ID and Shared ID guidance](https://help.impact.com/en/support/solutions/articles/48001249593-sub-ids-and-shared-ids-explained) supports click/action correlation; verify implementation and reporting access before attributing any sale.
