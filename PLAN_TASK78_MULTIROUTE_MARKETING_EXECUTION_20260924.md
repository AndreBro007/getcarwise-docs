# Task #78 — GetCarWise / CarClever multi-route marketing execution plan
**Decision version:** 24 September 2026. **Status:** prepared for André's review; no campaign, spend, publication or production change authorized. This is the execution synthesis of the completed strategy phases, not a rewrite of their evidence.

## 1. Commercial thesis and buyer promise

GetCarWise helps a US car buyer decide where to start; CarClever carries the buyer through live choices, comparison, risk flags and estimated costs before dealer contact. The old live ChatGPT @CarClever already provides the substantial Used/CPO journey and improved Recommended ranking. The pending New & Used/Claude variants extend reach and new-car coverage; do not advertise pending availability as live. The defensible claim is the connected journey, not an unverified superiority ranking from the August future-app competitor scorecard.

**Three brand jobs:** GetCarWise = independent owned advice and decision pages; CarClever = named guided app family; Edmunds = clearly identified external inventory/dealer or trade-in action, where the person chooses it and the source/placement is permitted. Brand awareness and useful product use have value even when the person does not submit a partner lead.

## 2. The end of the funnel can be reached by different journeys

| Journey | Buyer behavior | Candidate acquisition |
|---|---|---|
| Long | Relevant GetCarWise page → CarClever search/check/compare → optional labelled Edmunds handoff | Search, editorial/SEO, publisher referral |
| Medium | Relevant GetCarWise page → contextual New, Used or Trade-in Edmunds action | Search, permitted organic |
| Product-led | CarClever directory/deep link → guided journey → optional labelled handoff | App discovery, app-focused Search, permitted referral |
| Short | Channel-permitted Edmunds approved tracking asset → applicable lead form | Only where that channel and property are expressly allowed; **direct PPC→Edmunds and social→Edmunds are excluded under current supplied terms** |

The last step is an *optional end*, not a mandatory detour or a marketing prerequisite. We should seek shorter legal routes if a different channel/program allows them, without masking traffic origin. New Vehicle Lead and Used Vehicle Lead pay US$10 per **approved** item; Trade-in Offers Lead pays US$3.50. Their jobs and qualification forms differ; offer a CTA only when relevant.

## 4. Creative and positioning

**Primary used-buyer message:** “Compare used-car choices, review risk flags and estimated costs before contacting a dealer.” Show the stages with one real, accurate workflow; distinguish listing data from estimates and disclose limitations. For a person who does not know where to start: budget/needs → live shortlist → compare trade-offs → review risk and ownership cost → choose whether to contact a dealer. CarClever is consistent across old and newer apps, but ads must name only currently live capabilities. GetCarWise is the source of buyer education, not an Edmunds-branded proxy. Approved Edmunds creative may be displayed and linked as allowed; no prohibited Edmunds marks or automotive competitor/manufacturer claims in SEM or social copy.

Reddit messaging should be transparently founder-led, with André's role explicit. Check the existing account and ban scope first: username is fixed, display name/bio editable, and a replacement account must not evade a ban. Paid placement, if approved, is an ad with a useful demonstration; do not repeat the 81-post organic programme as the principal traffic engine.

## 5. Measurement contract: what can be learned quickly

Report **one row per source × landing/directory path × offer class × period**. Immediately: impressions, spend, clicks, consented engaged sessions, relevant page actions, app-link clicks, and tagged partner outbound. Where a platform exposes it, report real app use separately from site clicks. Later: Impact click, pending, accepted, rejected/reversed action and payout, with referral window/lock timing. Use distinct UTMs for campaigns, route-specific Impact-generated Sub IDs where supported, and a date/source register. Do not alter or mask the required Impact link, expose VIN/ZIP/personal details in tags, or claim that a no-referrer click is an app user.

**Limits:** historical Auto.dev requests are dominated by our development/testing. Pause discretionary owner-generated traffic through Sep 30 and use natural deltas only as a weak signal. Recent Impact 18 clicks/zero actions and 17 no-referrer clicks cannot prove source or failure. Two real CJ leads last month do not establish a scalable paid conversion rate. A paid Search test can show cost and useful visits in days; accepted-lead economics mature later under 30-day last-click, reversal and delayed locking. Do not present a Sep 30 paid test as final ROI.

## 6. Cash, credits and stop arithmetic

No media spend is approved. Set André's weekly hours and acceptable one-off loss separately from platform savings at the Sep 30 gate. A proposed **US$150 total** seven-day Google learning test is illustrative, not a commitment; at US$10 per accepted Used lead it needs **15 accepted leads** just to match media cost, before overhead. Hypothetical break-even CPC is US$0.20, $0.50, or $1.00 if 2%, 5%, or 10% of paid clicks become approved leads, respectively. Actual CPC and conversion are unknown; low traffic may make a seven-day test inconclusive. A brand/app-awareness test uses cost per meaningful app action, not affiliate break-even.

Google first-advertiser credits are account-specific and generally follow qualifying spend; an actual US$100–150 match has **not** been verified. Check offer, start/deadline, account country/currency and eligibility read-only before account creation, which may start a short clock. Reddit's current public offer is spend **US$500 in 14 days** to unlock US$500 future credit for eligible new advertisers; this is a separate cash-risk decision and not a reason to increase a small pilot to US$500. Do not count future credit as upfront financing.

Existing monthly cost baseline is about A$708; lean about A$208 retains paid Fractal. About A$150 requires a real no-cost Fractal production tier or approved exit and can break the old app/page 239. Shared Auto.dev Free 1,000 calls/month and truthful fallback need separate technical verification. Do not fund media from hypothetical savings before actual bills and dependency choices.

## 7. Rules that shape route selection

The supplied Edmunds agreement §3.A disallows direct PPC to Edmunds and restricts paid keywords, negatives and copy; §3.B disallows social Approved Ads or otherwise driving social traffic to Edmunds, including a disguised social→site→Edmunds route, unless Edmunds grants express written property permission for commission. §4 requires approved, unmodified creative/tracking; eligibility and US traffic/form requirements also apply. André clarified that the business operates from the US even though he resides in Australia; his residence alone is not a reason to reject the programme. Impact Help Center's generic social-sharing, Assets and campaign features do not override this advertiser contract. Market GetCarWise and CarClever for their own utility on social, but check the complete live app journey and do not promise social-origin Edmunds revenue without approval. No outgoing partner request is included in this plan.
