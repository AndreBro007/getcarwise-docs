## Key finding 1 — 3-row SUV page is the strongest GEO proof point

`/tools/best-3-row-suv-under-50000/` is the standout content page with 78 US Generative AI impressions in 28 days.

This is especially important because the page is also one of the existing SEO opportunity pages. It provides an internal GetCarWise example to study before making broad GEO assumptions.

Current visible characteristics include:
- explicit 2026 framing in the title;
- a clear ranking structure;
- numeric price, cargo, fuel-economy, reliability and warranty data;
- named best-use cases and watch-outs;
- comparison table;
- concise deal guidance.

Recommendation: use this page as an internal comparison template for content architecture, while improving its sourcing/freshness rather than mechanically copying wording.

## Key finding 2 — the `/tools/` hub has strong AI visibility

The tools hub received 50 US Generative AI impressions. This indicates Google's AI surfaces are not restricted to long editorial articles; well-connected navigational/explanatory hub pages can also be surfaced.

Implication: internal-link architecture and descriptive hub copy matter for GEO as well as conventional crawling/discovery.

## Key finding 3 — homepage is already visible

The homepage received 28 US impressions. Brand/entity-level visibility is therefore already present to some degree.

The site does not need a special AI-only technical layer to become eligible. Current Google Search Console settings confirm the property's Search generative AI control is **Include** via inherited default.

## Key finding 4 — $30k SUV page is underperforming in AI relative to its SEO opportunity

`/tools/best-compact-suv-under-30000/` received only **3 US Generative AI impressions** during the 28-day period.

This contrasts with Ubersuggest previously reporting the page around position #13 for `best suv under 30000` with approximately 880 US searches/month.

Interpretation: the page is a strong conventional SEO opportunity but has not yet translated that visibility into meaningful AI-feature exposure. That strengthens the case for the P1 refresh centred on:
- current 2026 evidence;
- proprietary market data;
- transparent methodology;
- explicit sourcing;
- refreshed new/used candidates;
- stronger internal-link context.

## Key finding 5 — recent growth is real, but currently plateaued

Daily Generative AI impressions stepped up materially around the end of August / beginning of September.

- First half of period: average ~3.9/day.
- Second half: average ~9.6/day.
- Latest 7 days vs previous 7 days: 68 vs 67.

This means visibility has improved, but the last two weekly blocks are effectively flat. Future changes should be measured against this baseline rather than assuming the early-period growth will continue automatically.
