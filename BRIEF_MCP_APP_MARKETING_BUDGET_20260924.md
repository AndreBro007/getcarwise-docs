## The commercial constraint

CJ showed two non-owner leads (US$20 total) according to André, with source unattributed. Impact currently shows 18 mixed clicks and no actions during its recent switch; 17 “no referring domain” clicks are source-unknown and cannot be credited to MCP. These counts do not establish an approved-lead conversion rate for an ad, app, or page.

Impact New/Used pays US$10 per approved lead and Trade-in US$3.50 under the recorded agreement. At a US$150 campaign cost, **15 approved New/Used leads** (or about 43 Trade-in leads) merely repay media, before time/other costs. The break-even CPC for New/Used is US$10 multiplied by the *approved-lead-per-paid-click* rate: 5% → US$0.50, 10% → US$1.00. These conversion rates are illustrative, not observed. A paid campaign can be an explicitly capped learning expense, but is not currently supported as a profitable acquisition engine.

OpenAI's current [publication and distribution requirements](https://developers.openai.com/plugins/deploy/app-review) permit discovery by exact-name search or direct link, while proactive recommendations are discretionary enhanced distribution. [Claude's connector guidance](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11176164-use-connectors-to-extend-claude-s-capabilities) places connection/enabling with the user. Marketing must explain the concrete buyer job and make installation/connection easy; it cannot purchase platform auto-trigger placement. Claude/Muse submissions remain pending and should not be advertised as available.

## Where effort would go after the pause

1. **Present the live app as a specific action:** “find a suitable car near your ZIP and check a dealer route,” with accurate scope. Put one direct directory link near a relevant owned decision, not a generic “AI car app” campaign. This is a future edit requiring André's approval. The pending V2 should use its own truthful two-tool proposition if approved.
2. **Use existing intent, not a broad audience:** the budget-SUV and used-listing questions already observed in GSC are candidates for contextual entry. A visitor who wants a specific shortlist is closer to an app connection or lead than a broad “car shopping” audience.
3. **Prefer permission-based distribution:** a genuinely useful response in one relevant community or a small partner introduction can test buyer language. No automated posts, paid placements, or outreach before approval; community rules and disclosure govern.
4. **If buying traffic later, separate acquisition from revenue:** an ad-to-directory click may not reveal whether the user connected or later invoked the app. A tracked owned landing can measure click-through but still cannot invent platform install data. If this gap remains, no revenue claim or performance scaling follows.

## Go/no-go for any paid test

After Sep 30 and after André decides the hours/cash envelope: (a) the advertised app and directory link are publicly live and useful, (b) destination/affiliate paths and disclosures are verified without contaminating the passive period, (c) at least landing→outbound and Impact accepted-action reporting work, (d) the maximum loss, daily limit, one audience and stop date are approved in writing, and (e) forecast paid CPC is compared with observed or conservative approved-lead conversion. If any gate fails, stay at US$0 media. An observed zero approved actions over a tiny test is an inconclusive result, but spend must still stop at the cap.
