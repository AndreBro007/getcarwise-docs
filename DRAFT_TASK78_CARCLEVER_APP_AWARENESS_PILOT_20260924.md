## 4. Success, stop and review

- **Operational pass:** signed-in listing link lands correctly; disclosure and copy are accurate; existing New/Used/Trade-in CTAs still work; one change and rollback are documented.
- **Early interest:** source-specific referred visits (where available), eligible page sessions and measured app CTA clicks are recorded. A nonzero rate is directional only; no numeric lift target can be defended until baseline is known.
- **Commercial proof:** a later accepted partner action can be attributed to this route only if the chain actually reconciles. Do not use the two historical CJ leads or the 17 no-referrer Impact clicks as pilot outcomes.
- **Stop/rollback:** wrong app or generic-directory destination for signed-in visitors, misleading product claims, broken existing CTA, trust complaint, or analytics not fit for the claimed metric. If traffic is too small, extend observation or mark inconclusive rather than creating more placements.
- **Review:** first at 14 days after approved launch for route health and qualified clicks; then at 28 days for mature actions, with a record of reporting lag.
