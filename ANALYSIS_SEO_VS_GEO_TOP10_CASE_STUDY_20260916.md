## Working hypothesis for the case study

The measured data suggests GetCarWise currently has two different visibility patterns:

1. **SEO demand pages** — budget/model/category guides can earn meaningful conventional impressions even while ranking too deep for clicks.
2. **GEO answer/entity pages** — clear explanatory, comparison, hub and product-understanding pages can earn disproportionate Generative AI visibility even without being the site's highest-volume conventional pages.

This is a working hypothesis, not proof of causation. Page structure, query mix, Google feature eligibility and searcher intent may all contribute.

## Implication for the next optimization round

Do not optimize every page with one template.

- For SEO-heavy pages: focus on ranking depth, freshness, unique market data, source quality, internal links and intent match.
- For GEO-heavy pages: preserve concise answer structure, comparison clarity, factual grounding, descriptive internal relationships and entity/product explanation.
- For pages appearing in both top tens: treat them as the best candidates for combined SEO + GEO optimization.
