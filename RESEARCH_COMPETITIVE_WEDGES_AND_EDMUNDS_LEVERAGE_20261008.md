# Competitive opportunities and Edmunds leverage

Date: 8 October 2026 (Brisbane).
Status: researched brainstorming and proposed learning direction. No new build, publication, campaign, spending, outreach or final product decision.

## Owner brief and strategic position

André wants marketing and buyer access to lead product selection. Dealer contact through an eligible Edmunds affiliate journey remains the commercial objective. The catalogue, Edmunds website and adjacent channels should reduce duplication and operating costs. He can invest substantial time but caps recurring spending at $200/month until progress is visible (A$200 is the working currency interpretation). Reported income is $20 and no real-user feedback is available.

Working position: win a specific buyer decision within a broader shopping journey. A buyer can discover cars elsewhere, then use GetCarWise to resolve the choice and prepare useful dealer questions. This is a candidate acquisition angle; it does not establish that competitors lack every constituent feature.

Do not assume the project can never succeed in SEO/GEO. Broad marketplace searches are an unattractive initial head-on contest; specific useful experiences can earn narrower visibility. “They do not cover it” must be checked per exact buyer task, not asserted from a product slogan.

## Three offers to investigate, in priority order

### 1. Shortlist rescue — “I have found the cars. Help me decide.”

**Who/when:** a buyer with two or three actual contenders, possibly from different marketplaces, who is stuck between them.

**Additional value to test:** relate the cars to the buyer's stated priorities; distinguish confirmed facts from unknowns; explain what would change the recommendation; prepare the specific questions needed before purchase. If a major requirement is missing, show a relevant alternative instead of another long result list.

**Acquisition angle:** Reddit shortlist discussions, relevant forums and specific search questions. Marketing speaks to the existing problem; the buyer keeps the work already done.

**How it might win a user from another service:** the source marketplace found an attractive car, but the buyer still wants a defensible choice across their contenders. They voluntarily bring their comparison task to us. This does not require intercepting competitor users or placing links in a marketplace without permission.

**Edmunds use:** catalogue examples/alternatives where supported; a suitable tracked actual listing if correspondence is established; otherwise a clearly labelled relevant search continuation. Do not substitute a different vehicle while implying it is the original contender.

**Contact motivation:** resolve full price, essential equipment, condition/records or inspection arrangements on the preferred actual car.

**First learning:** manually prepare a small set of complete briefs for consenting real buyers. Observe whether the explanation changes or clarifies their choice and whether they deliberately continue. Software extraction is not a prerequisite; links do not grant permission to scrape protected pages.

**Competitive gap status:** candidate workflow advantage, not proven exclusive. Cross-site comparison alone is already advertised elsewhere. Evidence, clarity, access, community usefulness and downstream completion must make the difference.

### 2. Same budget, different compromise — “Which extra cost is worth it for me?”

**Who/when:** someone deciding between an older hybrid and newer petrol vehicle, cheaper higher-mileage and dearer lower-mileage options, or used versus new under a real spending constraint.

**Additional value to test:** show the assumptions that could change the decision. Separate purchase-price evidence, buyer-supplied usage/cost inputs and uncertain future expenses. Explain when paying more for a feature or powertrain is worthwhile for that person's use.

**Acquisition angle:** specific decision questions in Search, a community case and a short visual/video explanation. Example query language is hypothetical, not measured keyword demand: “Is the hybrid premium worth it at my annual mileage?”; “Older hybrid versus newer petrol SUV at this budget?”

**How it might win a user:** generic deal ratings or comparison articles have not resolved the buyer's particular trade-off. We translate the comparison into an individual decision and a useful verification step.

**Edmunds use:** catalogue price examples as dated asking prices; link to appropriate cost/pricing tools and research where useful. No assumed free programmatic access to proprietary True Cost to Own values or editorial content.

**Contact motivation:** obtain real itemised prices for the finalists, or confirm the equipment that justifies the premium. A provisional comparison can explain exactly why the dealer's answer matters.

**First learning:** one focused comparison experience using clearly stated inputs and a matching buyer case. Check whether buyers vary the inputs, identify a preference and request a next step.

**Competitive gap status:** ownership-cost tools and price advice already exist. The hypothesis is that a focused personalised comparison and contact progression can be more helpful at this moment.

### 3. Choose together — “We want different things. Find the workable choice.”

**Who/when:** a household whose buyers disagree about space, appearance, comfort, fuel economy, age, price or ownership concerns.

**Additional value to test:** capture both sets of priorities; identify shared requirements and negotiable preferences; show the two or three defensible compromises; provide a portable brief both people can understand.

**Acquisition angle:** Reddit/Facebook discussions and partner/household sharing. The other person receives something directly relevant to their own purchase decision.

**How it might win a user:** a single-person filter or chat has not settled the household disagreement. We make the trade-off and shared decision explicit.

**Edmunds use:** appropriately supported examples and a continuation to view/compare real local options.

**Contact motivation:** both people want to test the preferred option, check seating/cargo/comfort, or confirm the actual total price.

**First learning:** one manually facilitated household case and a shareable summary. Observe whether the second person actually engages and whether they reach a practical next step.

**Competitive gap status:** promising collaborative framing; not established that no competitor offers similar functionality. Private household information should not become a public indexed asset by default.

### Later niche option: exact-configuration guidance

A model/year/package-specific decision sheet could be attractive in specialist communities, but only when reliable evidence supports the critical requirement. Towing, child-seat fit and safety must not be inferred from a broad model label or AI confidence. Cars.com's physical testing is a source to reference, not work we can claim to have performed.

## What “capture competitors' customers” means here

Capture a useful decision task rather than trying to move the entire shopping relationship at once. The buyer may continue using the original site. Our recommendation should remain honest when their existing contender is best or no relevant Edmunds alternative is found.

Show what we added: a clarified preference, a better comparison, a verified concern, a useful alternative, a jointly understood brief, or a dealer question worth asking. Sending the same user to another generic inventory homepage adds little.

Direct contact details on a competitor/private-seller listing do not establish a payable Edmunds lead. The commercial opportunity is an informed, relevant continuation through the supported affiliate journey. Do not distort a recommendation to produce commission.

## SEO/GEO angle selection

Look for combinations of decision stage + constraint + contenders + required next step. Avoid treating a narrow phrase as an unserved market merely because it sounds unusual.

Example candidate public experiences:
- Compare the older hybrid premium with a newer petrol option using stated personal usage.
- Compare two model years against a particular essential feature and budget.
- Prepare the questions that would settle a specific shortlist.
- Explain the spending/space compromise behind a household choice.

One useful, maintained experience can support several related questions. Do not generate a public page for every private brief or catalogue permutation. Test actual search results and search demand before a large content commitment; no volumes, ranking guarantees or traffic forecasts are offered here.

Community use can reveal buyers' own language quickly. Search performance has slower feedback. A community test cannot by itself prove organic Search acquisition, and ranking movement cannot prove dealer conversion.

## Measurement contract — proposed business requirements

Keep the offer version and source separate so we can diagnose a combination of buyer job and channel.

| Stage | Evidence available or required | What it answers |
|---|---|---|
| Relevant arrival | Source/campaign where available; search-query cohorts from Search Console | Does the promise reach people with the intended decision? |
| Real buyer starts | Buyer intentionally provides constraints/contenders; remove owner/test activity | Does the offer attract the right job? |
| Useful decision completed | Buyer reaches a brief/recommendation and voluntarily states what helped or remains unresolved | Is the experience useful? |
| Preferred car and contact reason | Explicit buyer selection of a contender and reason to continue | Have we created readiness rather than just engagement? |
| Tracked Edmunds continuation | Supported outbound tracking and cohort/placement labels | Did the handoff work? |
| Eligible submitted / approved action | Impact reporting, when available, reconciled to supported cohort data | Did commercial value occur? |

Our analytics cannot ordinarily observe Edmunds form starts, submissions, phone calls or messages directly. Do not pretend a GA event on our site proves dealer contact. An approved action is the commercial evidence; submission visibility and source granularity depend on the actual partner reporting available.

Use consistent counts and denominators: non-test starts, completions, handoffs and approved actions by source/offer/version. Define a repeat journey consistently. Avoid double-counting devices/sessions as unique people. Coarse approved placement labels suffice; do not put names, email addresses, VINs or raw buyer details into tracking IDs.

Record the inquiry motivation (availability, price, equipment, condition/inspection or practical fit), whether an exact or similar destination was used, and why the buyer declined when voluntarily given. Keep the brief private by default.

Record request/model costs and recurring bills against the owner's ceiling. No measured zero-cost claim is made for catalogue/API/model usage. Commission and variable cost must eventually be considered together.

## Proposed pivot logic

| Pattern | First change to investigate |
|---|---|
| Little relevant arrival | Audience access, channel or headline; do not infer product failure from no exposure. |
| Arrival but no start | Promise, relevance or unnecessary friction. |
| Starts but poor completion | Input burden, missing evidence, confusing output or lack of useful differentiation. |
| Completed briefs but no preferred choice | The comparison failed to resolve the decision, or the buyer is too early. |
| Clear choice/contact intent but little handoff | Suitable inventory, locality, destination continuity or contact framing. |
| Handoffs but no attributable action after the applicable delay | Actual destination, eligibility, attribution and the buyer's remaining reason not to contact. |
| Approved actions repeat with acceptable cost | Repeat the winning job/source combination before broadening. |

Change one major element at a time and keep a version/date record. A handful of buyers is qualitative learning, not statistical proof. Do not compare tiny mixed cohorts as if an A/B winner is established. Owner activity and deliberate link validation are separately labelled.

Small learning can begin through permitted real-buyer examples without a new expensive app or paid traffic. No exact pilot sample size, performance threshold or media spend is chosen by this research. Agree those against the actual reachable cohort before execution. Attribution/reporting delays mean rapid learning about usefulness is possible sooner than a confident revenue judgment.

## Where our speed can help

We can rapidly prepare a narrow experience, hear a real buyer's confusion, change the explanation and reuse it in native channel formats. Larger services already use AI and can copy simple features. Speed becomes valuable when it compounds into reliable evidence, useful partnerships/community trust and accumulated knowledge of real buyer decisions.

The proposed first learning priority is shortlist rescue; personalised compromise analysis is the second offer and shared household choice the third. These may share a buyer brief later; no common-engine architecture or build mandate is created.
