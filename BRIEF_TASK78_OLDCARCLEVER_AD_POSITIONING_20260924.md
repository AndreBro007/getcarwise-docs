# Task #78 — Old CarClever differentiation for Google Search

**Date:** 2026-09-24. **Status:** read-only positioning brief; no ad or site change. Built from the August Library competitive report, current GitHub submission/test/cost records, and existing live GetCarWise pages. This is an execution supplement, not a rewrite of the completed four strategy phases.

## Evidence boundary

The August `carclever_chatgpt_competitive_report_ai_readable.md` primarily scores the *future-state CarClever – New & Used Cars* against CARFAX, CarGurus, Autotrader and CarMax. That Fractal all-inventory product was withdrawn from OpenAI. André clarified that **the old live Used/CPO @CarClever has substantially the same buying tools**, while the newer app added new-vehicle inventory; old CarClever's Recommended ordering was also improved. The August 10/10 scores are historical, not current verified competitor rankings. Its full decision-journey assessment applies directionally to positioning the live old app.

The old live app's actual workflow is documented on the current [Try CarClever](https://getcarwise.app/try-carclever/) page, the old app's Sep 14 OpenAI resubmission and 20-row live search regression, and the worked August [user guide](https://getcarwise.app/carclever-guide/). It covers:
1. natural-language **used/CPO** inventory search and Deal Score;
2. listing detail and stated risk flags, with title/history gaps made explicit;
3. estimated payment and ownership cost, with assumptions;
4. side-by-side comparison and a **conversation-scoped** Garage shortlist;
5. referral to the Edmunds listing/dealer when appropriate.

This is a broad connected workflow for a buyer weighing an actual purchase. It is an **internal historical competitive assessment**, not a verified claim that current competitors lack these features or that CarClever is uniquely/officially superior. CARFAX has strong first-party history and filters; CarGurus has conversational search; Autotrader strong structured search; CarMax a transactional retailer. Do not challenge them on their strongest proprietary evidence or claim that CarClever supplies an independent history report or financing offer.

## Message hierarchy for the paid test

**Buyer problem:** “I do not know where to start with a used car, or which shortlist option is worth pursuing and affordable.”

**Promise:** “Compare used-car choices, review risk flags and estimated costs before contacting a dealer.”

**Proof:** show one short, truthful path from an existing live test: search → one listing's Deal Score/risk → two-car comparison or cost estimate → labelled dealer destination. Screenshots/examples must be checked against the post-Sep 30 surviving product. Keep the ad itself narrower than the page.

**Provisional ad theme (for account preview, not publication):**
- Headline candidates: “Check a Used Car Before You Buy”; “Compare Used Car Choices”; “Review Risk & Ownership Cost”; “CarClever in ChatGPT”.
- Description candidates: “Search U.S. used listings and compare price, risk flags and estimated costs.” / “See the trade-offs before contacting a dealer. Open CarClever in ChatGPT.”
- Keyword hypothesis: exact-match `[check used car deal]`, `[compare used cars]`, `[used car buying assistant]`; final terms require real Keyword Planner volume/CPC and search-term/SEM restriction review. Avoid implying a free Carfax/history report, inspection, guaranteed price, live financing offer or automatic ChatGPT invocation.

## Destination choice

**First candidate if old Fractal is retained:** [Try CarClever](https://getcarwise.app/try-carclever/), because it describes the live broad workflow and links to the ChatGPT app. A paid click to this page can measure arrival and a subsequent app-directory click, subject to GA4/consent verification. It cannot by itself prove app connection, tool use or approved Edmunds lead. To make this a *monetization* test, verify an actual source marker survives the old app → Impact click/action chain; otherwise describe it only as paid app-interest research and do not report cost per lead.

**If old Fractal is retired or reduced so the promise is no longer true:** do not run this ad. The previous $30k SUV guide/Page 827 and Lite V2 are a **different, narrower campaign** requiring revised copy, destination and measurement approval, not a silent redirect.

**Existing guide risk:** `/carclever-guide/` is a worked Aug 2 example and currently says the app covers new/demo inventory and VIN-specific recalls in places. That conflicts with the old live Used/CPO scope and the current `/try-carclever/` page's model-level recall disclosure. Do not buy traffic to the guide or quote those claims until a separately approved editorial correction. The current `/try-carclever/` page itself requires a quick post-Sep 30 check against the real hosting/tier and exact app link.

## Sep 30 dependency

Auto.dev Free preserves listings/photos/VIN decode, but its Growth-only Specs/Recalls/APR/Payments/TCO data may be unavailable; the proposed fallback estimates and model-level recall must remain clearly labelled. Fractal cancellation ends the old app. A Sep 24 owner/Fractal Free simulation reported an empty Vehicle Details view, still under investigation. Therefore do not advertise “full specs,” VIN-specific recalls, “real lender rates” or an unbroken detail flow until the cost configuration and one narrow post-pause verification are settled. The current page promises estimated costs and model-level recalls; the ad should stay within that truthful scope.

## Decision

This evidence materially improves the ad **positioning**, but also changes the landing and measurement gate in `DRAFT_TASK78_GOOGLE_SEARCH_LEARNING_TEST_20260924.md`. The first paid experiment should sell the decision job rather than generic SUV rankings **only if** the old app survives Sep 30 and paid source→Impact action attribution is verifiable. Otherwise the narrower website/Impact route is the monetization test. No spend or publication is approved here.
