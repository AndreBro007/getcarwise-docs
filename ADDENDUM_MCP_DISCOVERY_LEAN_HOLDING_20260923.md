# MCP discovery and low-cost holding option — 2026-09-23

**Scope:** Read-only decision addendum to the completed marketing/revenue strategy. No application, submission, budget, service plan or production change. This note records new primary-source platform evidence; it does not rewrite the four completed phases.

## Decision-relevant platform facts

- **ChatGPT/OpenAI:** The Dec 2025 announcement described @ mention or tool-menu invocation after connection, while contextual surfacing was explicitly experimental. The current published plugin review requirements say a published plugin is findable by exact-name search or direct directory link. Main-page placement and proactive suggestions are enhanced distribution; *few plugins receive it at publication*, and developers cannot request it. Approval and publication are separate steps. It is therefore unsafe to assume old CarClever's low use proves a broken automatic trigger, competitor capture, or weak buyer demand. It may simply not receive proactive distribution. [OpenAI announcement](https://openai.com/index/developers-can-now-submit-apps-to-chatgpt/), [current distribution requirements](https://developers.openai.com/plugins/deploy/app-review).
- **Claude:** Claude says it may suggest a **connected** app contextually without the user naming it; the user must connect and enable the connector for the conversation. When multiple connected apps fit, Claude can offer a choice. This is a meaningful capability, but directory approval does not itself mean every Claude user has connected GetCarWise, and no traffic guarantee follows. The GetCarWise submission remains pending per current project record. [Claude suggestions](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/14730684-how-claude-suggests-connected-apps), [connector setup](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11176164-use-connectors-to-extend-claude-s-capabilities).
- **Tool selection:** OpenAI's metadata guide says model tool selection depends on names, descriptions and parameter docs. It recommends labelled direct, indirect and negative prompt tests and weekly tool-call analytics. This can diagnose behavior for an installed/connected app; a developer test cannot measure population-level proactive exposure. [OpenAI metadata guidance](https://developers.openai.com/plugins/guides/optimize-metadata).
- **Unknowns specific to CarClever:** We have no platform-level counts of eligible conversations, directory views, connection rate, contextual suggestions or competitor share. The two historical completed leads are unattributed. Do not assign a cause to low traffic from this evidence alone. The old live app, pending Find My Car V2 on OpenAI/Claude, and pending Muse connector are distinct cohorts and endpoints.

## Decision at Sep 30

A **lean holding mode** is a defensible option if the apps remain reachable, tool reliability and lead-link attribution survive the lower-cost configuration, and the fixed burn fits André's envelope. Keep just enough to maintain pending reviews, monitor weekly and respond to approval or failures. The previously drafted ~A$150/month lean figure is conditional, not a verified achievable bill; the recorded August baseline was about A$708/month with Auto.dev Growth A$469 the largest line. A plan downgrade without dependency testing risks turning a distribution wait into a nonfunctional app.

Do not make “wait for automatic trigger to mature” the sole revenue plan. OpenAI's current requirements make proactive placement discretionary; Claude's contextual suggestion applies to connected users. A minimal owned-site presence and one working attribution path are useful low-cost fallbacks and ways to seed direct app installs, while larger editorial production and paid campaigns can be paused. The Sep 30 decision should compare three explicit choices: (A) keep current stack briefly with evidence of near-term use, (B) tested lean holding mode, or (C) retire the old Fractal-dependent surface while preserving pending V2/Claude/Muse where technically and commercially viable. Approval of any subscription or production change remains André's decision.
