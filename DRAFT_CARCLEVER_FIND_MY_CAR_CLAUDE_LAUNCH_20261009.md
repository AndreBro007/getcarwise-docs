# CarClever – Find My Car Is Now Available on Claude

**Review draft — prepared 9 October 2026; not published.** Confirm the public Claude destination in a browser before publication. WordPress-ready body: `DRAFT_CARCLEVER_FIND_MY_CAR_CLAUDE_LAUNCH_20261009.html`.

CarClever – Find My Car is now published in Claude’s connector directory. You can search U.S. new and used vehicle listings from a conversation, refine what you are looking for, and open a promising vehicle on Edmunds to check availability and contact the dealer.

Already use Find My Car in ChatGPT? You can now choose the assistant you prefer.

## Start with the car you need

Tell CarClever your budget, U.S. ZIP code and requirements. For example: “Use CarClever – Find My Car to find a Honda CR-V under $30,000 near 90210.”

Review the returned vehicles, then adjust the search as you learn what matters. You might ask for lower-mileage options, a different model or a wider search area. Results depend on current listings and the information available for each vehicle.

## Connect CarClever in Claude

1. Open [CarClever – Find My Car in the Claude directory](https://claude.ai/directory/carclever-find-my-car).

1. Review its description and choose Connect or Install, following Claude’s prompts. You can also browse under Customize → Connectors.

1. Make sure CarClever is enabled for your conversation, then ask it to search with your budget and U.S. ZIP code.

CarClever requires no separate account or sign-in. You still need your Claude account and access to connectors; organisation settings may affect availability. The listing is a Community connector.

## Turn a shortlist into a useful next step

When a vehicle looks worth pursuing, open its Edmunds link. Confirm that it is still available and ask the dealer about the details that could change your decision, such as the final price, equipment, warranty or available history records.

Missing information remains unconfirmed. A search result does not guarantee a vehicle’s condition, price or availability.

## Which CarClever experience does this announcement cover?

This announcement covers CarClever – Find My Car, the conversational vehicle-search app. It is separate from our earlier used-car buying toolkit and from the website’s Deal Score and Price Check tools. A Match Score in Find My Car describes how a vehicle fits your request; it is not the website calculator’s price-value Deal Score.

See the [Find My Car guide](https://getcarwise.app/carclever-find-my-car/) for supported examples and setup.
