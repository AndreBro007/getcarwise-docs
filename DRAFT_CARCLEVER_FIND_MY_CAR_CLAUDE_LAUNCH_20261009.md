## Where CarClever fits in your car-buying journey

Buying a car rarely starts with a perfectly specified search. You might know your budget but be unsure which models fit your family. Or you may already have two favourites and need to find actual vehicles worth pursuing.

Find My Car helps at the point where research needs to become a practical shortlist.

**Explore:** Start with your budget, U.S. ZIP code and the job the car needs to do. Ask Claude to use CarClever to search, rather than relying only on general model advice.

**Narrow:** Review the returned vehicles. Tell Claude what you like, what does not fit and which requirements are essential. Ask it to refine the search with CarClever.

**Prepare to contact:** Choose a promising listing, open its Edmunds link and identify the questions you want answered before arranging a visit.

**Verify with the dealer:** Check availability, the written out-the-door price, equipment and relevant records. For a used vehicle, arrange an independent inspection before committing.

You do not have to finish every decision before starting. The conversation gives you somewhere to work through those decisions.

## Your budget is the start of the conversation

“Find a large SUV under $80,000” leaves several choices open. Do you want the cheapest suitable SUV, the newest one you can afford, or a lower-mileage example with particular equipment?

A $33,000 SUV can meet an $80,000 price ceiling and still be far from what you intended.

Tell Claude what the budget means to you: “I can spend up to $80,000, but I’m looking for newer, lower-mileage options—not simply the cheapest large SUVs. Use CarClever – Find My Car and explain the trade-offs.”

Being explicit helps turn a broad search into a useful one. If the first results miss your intention, say what needs to change and ask for another search.

## From “that looks interesting” to a useful dealer conversation

A good next step is specific. Instead of contacting a dealer with a vague enquiry, use your shortlist to identify what could change your decision.

For example:

- “Is this exact vehicle still available, and can you confirm the VIN?”
- “Can you send the written out-the-door price, including fees and any required add-ons?”
- “Does this vehicle have the equipment I need?”
- “For this used car, which service and history records are available, and can I arrange an independent inspection?”

Find My Car links open on Edmunds, where you can review the listing and use the available dealer-contact options. Your enquiry is an opportunity to confirm the facts before spending time on a visit.

## Questions buyers ask

### Can I search for both new and used cars?

Yes. Find My Car searches U.S. new and used vehicle inventory. Include your preferred condition in the request, or say that you want to consider both.

### Is this the same as the original CarClever toolkit?

This launch is for CarClever – Find My Car. Our earlier used-car toolkit and the website’s evaluation tools remain separate options for other parts of the buying journey. Find My Car’s Match Score describes how a vehicle fits your request; it is different from a price-value Deal Score.

### Does a listing confirm availability or vehicle history?

No. Confirm availability and the final price with the dealer. Unreported accident, ownership or certification information remains unconfirmed. A listing or match score does not replace a history report or an independent inspection.

### Can I also use CarGurus in Claude?

Yes. [CarGurus also offers a Claude connector](https://claude.com/marketplace/connectors/cargurus). You can explore more than one source. When comparing results, use the same location and requirements, and distinguish a maximum price from a preference for newer vehicles closer to that budget.
