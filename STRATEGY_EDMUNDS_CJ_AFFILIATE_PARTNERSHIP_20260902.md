# Edmunds Partnership and CJ Affiliate Website Strategy — 2026-09-02

## Strategic premise

Use the Edmunds relationship as a three-part growth asset:

1. **Revenue:** send high-intent users to relevant Edmunds destinations and measure qualified leads.
2. **User utility:** make GetCarWise pages more complete at the exact moment a shopper needs the next decision.
3. **Partnership development:** use real traffic, lead, and conversion evidence to justify a stronger relationship with Edmunds—not assume that affiliate links themselves create backlinks or ranking authority.

Affiliate outbound links are not backlinks to GetCarWise. They point away from the site, and commercial affiliate links should be clearly disclosed and qualified appropriately. SEO upside should come indirectly from better task completion, stronger topical usefulness, better internal linking, and content that earns genuine citations or links from other sites. Do not publish large numbers of thin Edmunds links for perceived SEO benefit.

## Recommended website architecture

### 1. “Next best action” modules

Add a small, clearly labelled next-step module to relevant pages:

- after a used-car evaluation: “Browse comparable used cars on Edmunds”
- after a vehicle valuation article: “See what your car may be worth”
- before selling: “Get an Edmunds offer for your car”
- after a new-car buying guide: “Check new-car quotes and incentives”
- after ownership-cost content: “Review Edmunds’ cost-of-ownership estimates”

Each module should have one primary CTA, one sentence of context, and a disclosure. Avoid presenting multiple competing Edmunds links in the same block.

### 2. Intent-matched editorial hubs

Build or improve pages around genuine search intent, then place the relevant Edmunds destination within the decision flow:

- Best SUVs under $30,000
- Hybrid buying guides
- Used-car buying guides
- How much is my car worth?
- Sell or trade in my car
- New-car incentives and rebates
- Cost of owning a car

The editorial page should answer the user’s question independently. Edmunds is the next action for listings, quotes, valuation, or deeper research—not the substance copied onto the page.

### 3. Find My Car handoff

Once Find My Car is approved and live, use its outbound-link model as the primary handoff:

1. GetCarWise evaluates and narrows the choice.
2. The result gives a concise reason to trust the match.
3. Edmunds receives the qualified next click for listing, quote, valuation, or ownership research.

Do not make the website depend on Find My Car before approval. During the transition, keep the legacy ChatGPT path accurate and avoid promoting the withdrawn New & Used Cars app.

## “Working closer with Edmunds” plan

The strongest path to a closer relationship is evidence, not link volume.

### Build a 30-day partner evidence pack

Track by page and link:

- impressions and organic clicks;
- page/query intent;
- outbound Edmunds clicks;
- CJ leads and EPC;
- destination type;
- device and country where available;
- broken or irrelevant destinations;
- assisted conversions where measurable.

After 30 days, produce a short partner report showing which user intents and placements generate qualified activity.

### Ask Edmunds/CJ for a partner conversation

Use the evidence pack to request:

- a named affiliate/account contact;
- confirmation of the best-performing creative and destinations for GetCarWise’s audience;
- approval or guidance for deep-linking to high-intent contextual destinations;
- access to current creative, tracking, and attribution recommendations;
- clarification on whether Edmunds offers co-marketing, editorial collaboration, research access, or partner amplification for high-quality publishers;
- a review of whether any API/data partnership is available through an account executive.

Do not ask Edmunds to link to GetCarWise as an SEO favour. Ask for a legitimate partnership, co-marketing, data, or distribution opportunity grounded in demonstrated qualified traffic.

### Create something worth Edmunds referencing

Potential assets:

- an independent “car-shopping decision” methodology page;
- a transparent comparison of search, valuation, incentives, and ownership-cost journeys;
- quarterly market-intent reports based on aggregated GetCarWise search demand;
- a methodology or consumer education resource Edmunds can cite or share if it is genuinely useful.

These assets can earn real backlinks from automotive writers, dealers, educators, and industry publications. The value comes from original analysis and usefulness, not from linking to Edmunds.

## Compliance and trust rules

- Clearly disclose affiliate relationships near relevant CTAs.
- Use the correct CJ tracking URL; do not manually rewrite or obscure it.
- Qualify commercial affiliate links with the appropriate sponsored treatment.
- Do not imply Edmunds endorses GetCarWise unless Edmunds explicitly grants permission.
- Do not copy Edmunds editorial content, ratings, data, logos, or claims without the required permission or approved creative.
- Validate every destination before publishing and remove dead or irrelevant links.
- Prefer one relevant link over a link catalogue.
- Keep the page useful if the visitor does not click.

## Recommended sequence

1. Start with Tier 1 links on existing high-intent pages.
2. Run a 30-day click/lead test with clean page-level attribution.
3. Use the zero-click SUV SEO rewrite as the first implementation surface.
4. Add valuation/sell/trade-in modules to relevant existing pages.
5. Prepare the Edmunds partner evidence pack.
6. Approach Edmunds/CJ for a strategic partner conversation.
7. After Find My Car approval, connect its evaluated results to the most relevant Edmunds destinations and re-measure.

## Bottom line

Edmunds should function as the trusted next step in the GetCarWise journey: GetCarWise helps the user decide what to do; Edmunds helps them act. The near-term win is better-qualified clicks and leads. The longer-term SEO and partnership win comes from original, useful decision-support content that earns attention independently.
